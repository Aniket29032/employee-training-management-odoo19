# Employee Training Management

An Odoo 19 module for managing the complete employee training lifecycle, including courses, training sessions, trainers, employee attendance, training tasks, task reviews, certificates, and email notifications.

## ✨ Features

- 📚 Course Management
- 👨‍🏫 Trainer Management
- 👨‍💼 Employee Enrollment
- 📅 Training Session Scheduling
- 🕒 Attendance Tracking
- 📝 Training Task Assignment
- 🔄 Task Submission & Review
- ✅ Task Approval & Rejection
- 💬 Trainer Review Comments
- 🏆 Certificate Management
- 📄 PDF Certificate Generation
- 🔢 Automatic Certificate Numbering
- 📧 Automated Email Notifications
- 📎 Certificate PDF Email Attachments
- 💬 Odoo Chatter Integration
- 🔔 Activity & Notification Tracking
- 🔐 Role-Based Access Control
- ⚙️ Data Validation & Integrity
- 🔗 HR, Project & Mail Integration
- 🎨 Customized Odoo Views and Workflows
